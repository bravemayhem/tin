# Menstrual Device Duration Guide

| Type of Device     | Recommended Change/Empty Time | Absolute Maximum Wear Time |
|--------------------|-------------------------------|----------------------------|
| Disposable Pads    | Every 3–4 hours               | 8 hours (Overnight)        |
| Tampons            | Every 4–8 hours               | 8 hours (Strict limit)     |
| Menstrual Cups     | Every 8–12 hours              | 12 hours                   |
| Menstrual Discs    | Every 8–12 hours              | 12 hours                   |
| Period Underwear   | Every 8–12 hours              | 12 hours                   |
| Reusable Pads      | Every 4–6 hours               | 12 hours                   |
| Menstrual Sponges  | Every 3–6 hours               | 8 hours                    |

## Notes

- **Tampons** have a strict 8-hour maximum due to TSS (Toxic Shock Syndrome) risk.
- **Disposable Pads** max is typically overnight use only.
- Always follow your healthcare provider's advice.
